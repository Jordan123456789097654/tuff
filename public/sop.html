02

Zero-Float Cash Control ($0.00 Start), Float Building & Bleed

Strict $0.00 opening balance, organic cash float accumulation, and $150.00 cash drop rules

⚠️ Mandatory Zero-Float Policy ($0.00 Starting Cash)

To eliminate overnight theft vulnerability and streamline accounting, Jordan's Snack Shack registers ALWAYS open with exactly $0.00 physical cash float.

  • Opening Count: Register opening cash float is entered as $0.00.
  • Float Building: Change is made organically from early cash purchases ($1, $5 bills).
  • Exact Change & Digital Preference: Cashiers actively encourage student digital pass scans, Apple Wallet, or exact cash tenders during early operations.
  • Large Bill Restriction: Cashiers do not accept $50 or $100 bills without prior manager approval and sufficient drawer change accumulated.

💵 Counterfeit Detection Matrix

  • $20.00 Bills: Mandatory UV security strip check and portrait watermark inspection.
  • Suspect Notes: Hold bill, discreetly call Station Lead, and tag CCTV time-machine recording.

🔒 $150.00 Intermediate Cash Bleed

When physical cash in drawer reaches $\ge \$150.00$, transfer $50.00$ or $100.00$ in large bills into a tamper-evident Drop Envelope, record the drop in POS, and transfer directly to Locker Vault #314 drop slot.

03

Dual-Screen Topology, Zero-Latency Bus & Offline Resiliency

Local BroadcastChannel topology, Server-Sent Events (SSE) relay, and IndexedDB caching

The dual-screen architecture utilizes a primary BroadcastChannel('snack_shack_customer_display') bus for sub-millisecond local screen synchronization on single devices, complemented by background Server-Sent Events (SSE) on /api/display/events for cross-device tablet/phone tethering. In the event of total school Wi-Fi failure, all orders queue in local storage with automatic sync upon reconnect.

04

Customer CCTV Surveillance, Microphone Audio Recording & 24-Hour DVR

Customer-facing camera feed, live decibel meter, synchronized microphone audio recording, and 24-hour time-machine DVR

The 2nd Customer Display station maintains an active camera and microphone recording stream. Both high-definition video and synchronized ambient microphone audio are continuously buffered into the 24-Hour Rolling DVR Time-Machine (86,400 seconds) and streamed live to the Cashier POS Monitor.

  • Audio Recording: Captures counter dialogue, verbal change confirmations, and hostile volume spikes ($>75\text{ dB}$).
  • 24-Hour Timeline Scrubbing: Operators can scrub back across the entire 24-hour operational window using quick preset jump buttons (-24h, -12h, -4h, -1h, -30m, -5m, LIVE) or variable playback speeds (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x scan).
  • 10-Second A/V Clip Export: Cashiers or leads can instantly download 10-second synchronized video + audio clips directly from the display or cashier hub.
  • Cashier Webcam Protection: Cashier-facing webcams remain strictly unrecorded to safeguard student volunteer privacy.
05

Biometric Face ID, Apple Wallet & Printable 8-Up Badges

Digital pass issuance, 8-up sheet printing, and biometric facial scan authentication

Students can pay via physical 8-Up barcoded badges, PKPass Apple Wallet cards, or optional on-device Face ID vector verification. All facial recognition vectors are computed client-side and never transmitted or stored in raw image form.

Dynamic Weather-Triggered Smart Pricing Engine

Automated weather rules, cold drink heatwave discounts, and rainy day hot cocoa perks

The POS interfaces with Open-Meteo API. Temperature $>80^\circ\text{F}$ triggers automatic 25¢ discounts on cold hydration items (Powerade, Cold Water). Heavy rain triggers promotional discounts on hot snacks. Surge pricing is strictly prohibited by school charter.

Multi-Person Crowd Warning & Single-File Queue Control

When $>3$ persons cluster directly at Table 4B counter, the customer screen sounds a gentle chime and displays "Please Form a Single-File Queue Behind the Yellow Line". The Line Concierge steps in to direct flow.

Storage Locker Vault #314 & Cold Chain Logistics

Vault #314 combination custody, insulated cooler totes, and FIFO date rotation

All reserve inventory is secured in Locker Unit #314. Cold drinks are maintained in commercial insulated totes with food-grade gel ice packs. Stock is rotated strictly First-In, First-Out (FIFO). Any damaged or expired item is immediately logged in the Spoilage & Shrinkage ledger.

21

Employee Quality Control (QC Taste Test) & Sampling Governance

Volunteer snack taste-testing, crispiness & freshness verification, and automated comp inventory shrinkage logging

To ensure student customer satisfaction and verify product crispiness, seasoning balance, and beverage chill temperatures, designated volunteers and station leads are authorized to conduct Employee Quality Control (QC Taste Tests) under strict ledger accountability.

😋 Sampling Authorization & Limits

  • Max Allowance: 1 sampling unit per volunteer shift (e.g. 1 bag of chips or 1 cold drink).
  • Mandatory System Logging: Cashiers must immediately open the Employee Quality Control modal, select the item, input the volunteer name, select a verdict (e.g. Grade A+ Exceptional), and record sensory notes.
  • Auto-Deduction: The POS automatically updates warehouse and register stock to prevent unrecorded inventory drift.

📋 Audit Compliance & Reconciliation

All QC deductions are classified under official operational shrinkage (Employee Quality Control). They are included in the end-of-day Z-Report and Kyro AI Loss Prevention audits to ensure 100% transparent book balancing between physical inventory counts and financial receipts.
