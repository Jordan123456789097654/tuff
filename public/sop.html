Daily Schedule & Operating Window

Jordan’s Snack Shack operates strictly during the designated morning window prior to first period in Forsyth County:

  • 07:45 – 08:00 AM: Locker retrieval, table setup, and cash float count.
  • 08:00 – 08:44 AM (Live Sales): Main store operations, walk-in register sales, and pre-order queue pickups.
  • 08:45 AM: Complete drawer close, reconcile cash, and return secure lockbox to locker storage.
Rule: All store operations must halt cleanly at 08:44 AM to ensure punctuality for first period classes.
2

Morning Setup & Locker Restock

Follow these steps every morning before unlocking the cash register:

  1. Locker Storage Retrieval: Fetch the insulated drink tote, snack bins, and cash lockbox from the locker.
  2. Table Layout: Set up table bins categorized by price point:
    • $1.50 Tier: Standard chips (Doritos, Lay's, Cheetos, Funyuns).
    • $2.00 Tier: Takis Blue Heat / Fuego, full-size candy bars.
    • $2.45 Tier: Chilled Powerade and bottled beverages.
  3. Power On & Cash Float: Boot the POS laptop. Open the cash lockbox and verify the starting float (typically $20.00 to $40.00 in small bills and quarters). Record the starting cash in the Drawer / Shifts tab.

2nd Customer Screen Setup (Dual Monitor or iPad/Phone)

The system supports real-time synchronization between the cashier POS and a customer-facing display. You can run the customer display in two ways:

🖥️ Option A: Same Laptop / 2nd Monitor

Click the 2nd Screen button at the top of the POS header and select "Launch 2nd Window". Drag the new window to your external monitor. Updates sync instantly (0ms latency).

📱 Option B: iPad / Phone / 2nd Device

Click 2nd Screen in the POS header. Scan the dynamic QR code with the iPad or iPhone camera, or open the displayed URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.X:3000/display). Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.

Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Tip: If the school guest Wi-Fi blocks communication between devices, turn on your iPhone or Android Personal Hotspot and connect both the laptop and iPad to it.

POS Register & Checkout Workflows

The cashier dashboard is designed for high-speed transactions:

Adding Items to Cart:

Click any snack tile on the screen, or click Scan Barcode (or press external USB scanner) to scan product UPC codes. Items and combo discounts appear immediately on the customer screen.

Accepting Payment:

Select the customer's preferred payment method:

  • 💵 Cash: Click exact amount or use quick dollar buttons (+$1, +$5, +$10). The POS calculates exact change due.
  • 📱 Venmo / CashApp: Click "Pay QR" in the header to display your payment QR code on screen.
  • 💳 Student Prepaid Pass: Select student from list or scan their barcode to deduct from their account balance.
Punch Card & Free Snacks:

Every purchase gives the student 1 punch. Upon reaching 10 punches, a golden banner automatically appears awarding 1 Free Snack item.

Student Face ID & Pass Lookup

Students can check their balance or checkout without physical ID cards using the optical Face ID feature:

  • Cashier Lookup: Click "Face ID" on the cashier dashboard or click the camera scan button.
  • 2nd Monitor Customer Lookup: Customers can tap the "Face ID" button directly on the 2nd display tablet/screen. When recognized, it alerts the cashier and loads their student pass instantly.
  • Adding New Faces: In the Students & Badges tab, click on any student profile and use the webcam to capture 1 clear reference photo.

Instant Stealth Mode / School Assignment Disguise

If a teacher or school administrator approaches and you need to immediately mask the store interfaces, activate Stealth Mode. This instantly freezes transactions and swaps BOTH screens to look like standard school assignments:

POS Cashier Screen Disguise: Transforms into a Google Docs "AP Environmental Science Study Guide" with essay prompts and soil horizon tables.
Customer 2nd Screen Disguise: Transforms into the "Oakridge High School LMS Student Digital Reader" displaying Unit 4 Biology reading notes.
How to Trigger Stealth Mode:
Alt + S
F2 Key
3x Escape Tap

Hidden Click Trigger: Clicking the extreme top-left pixel/corner of either screen will also quietly trigger or dismiss the disguise.

End of Shift Drawer Reconciliation (08:45 AM)

At the conclusion of the operating window, reconcile cash before packing up:

  1. Click Close Register in the top header or navigate to the Drawer / Shifts tab.
  2. Count the actual cash bills and coins in the physical lockbox.
  3. Enter the counted total into the Ending Cash ($) field.
  4. The system calculates the expected total (Starting Float + Cash Sales) and indicates whether the drawer is Exact, Over, or Short.
  5. Click Reconcile & Close Shift. This archives the shift report and saves daily profits to the analytics log.
  6. Pack the lockbox and snack bins and return them securely to the locker storage.
