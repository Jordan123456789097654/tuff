⚠️ 8-Allergen Physical Isolation Matrix

To guarantee zero cross-contact between snack products, inventory inside Locker Vault #314 is strictly segregated into dedicated, color-coded, airtight bins:

  • Red Bin (High Alert): Peanut Butter Bars & Tree Nut Trail Mix.

🔍 4-Tier Counterfeit Interception Protocol

  • 1. Portrait Raised Texture: Rub fingernail over jacket of portrait to feel distinctive raised intaglio print.
  • 2. Watermark Light Test: Hold bill to overhead cafeteria light to reveal embedded portrait watermark.
  • 3. Color-Shifting Ink: Tilt $10, $20, or $50 bill to check numeric color shift from copper to green.
  • 4. Non-Confrontational Protocol: If bill fails, politely state: "Our register scanner is unable to verify this bill. Do you have an alternate bill or student pass?" Retain suspect note discreetly for supervisor inspection.
💰 Mid-Shift Cash Dropping & Bleed Rule

Whenever the physical currency in the cash drawer exceeds $150.00, the cashier must perform an intermediate cash bleed. Count out $50.00 or $100.00 in excess bills, seal into a tamper-evident Drop Envelope, sign the envelope seal, and deposit it into the drop slot of Locker Vault #314.

Customer CCTV Surveillance, 1-Hour DVR & Evidence Vault

Customer-isolated optical recording, time-machine scrubbing, and incident evidence logging

🛡️ Customer-Isolated Camera Principle (FERPA & COPPA Compliance)

To guarantee operator privacy and focus strictly on register security, the cashier terminal webcam is never activated or recorded. Surveillance capture originates solely from the forward-facing camera mounted on the Customer Screen (`/display`) at Table 4B. The Cashier POS functions strictly as a remote surveillance monitor and DVR playback viewer.

⏪ 1-Hour Rolling DVR Scrubber Engine

The cashier station continuously stores a 60-minute rolling buffer (dvrRollingBuffer) of timestamped customer video frames and counter audio levels.

  • Quick Jump Presets: Instant buttons for -1m, -5m, -15m, -30m, and -60m.
  • Variable Playback Speed: Smooth replay at 0.5x, 1.0x, 2.0x, or 4.0x scan.
  • Immediate Live Return: Clicking 🔴 GO LIVE resets offset to real-time.

Digital pass issuance, biometric luminance matching math, and printable PVC badges

👤 Biometric Optical Match Algorithm

When Face Scan is requested, the system samples a 120x120 grayscale luminance matrix from the Customer Screen camera:

// Luminance Calculation Formula:
Y = (0.299 * R) + (0.587 * G) + (0.114 * B)
MatchScore = 100 - (Sum(|Y_cam - Y_db|) / (120*120*255)) * 100

Threshold: Matches above 90% confidence trigger instant account selection in 0.2s.

Total Counted Cash in Box - Expected Cash = Discrepancy

⚖️ Discrepancy Action Thresholds

  • 🟢 $0.00 (Balanced): Perfect reconciliation. Print audit report.
  • 🔵 ± < $1.00 (Acceptable): Minor change rounding. Log audit note.
  • 🔴 > $2.00 (Escalation): Mandatory recount with supervisor. Check CCTV DVR replay for unlogged cash drops.

🔐 08:44 AM Vault Lockup & Equipment Storage

Hardware Troubleshooting Flowcharts & Decision Trees

Rapid resolution trees for screen disconnect, barcode scanner failure, and printer jams

🔴 2nd Screen Disconnected
  1. Check if Customer Screen browser tab is open at /display.
  2. Verify battery power & Wi-Fi indicator on tablet.
  3. Refresh /display tab; BroadcastChannel will re-pair in <1s.
🏷️ Barcode Scanner Jam / Miss
  1. Wipe optical glass lens with microfiber cloth.
  2. Re-seat USB-C OTG cable connection.
  3. Fallback: Type numeric student ID directly into search bar.
🖨️ Thermal Printer Paper Out
  1. Pop top release latch; insert fresh 58mm thermal roll.
  2. Ensure paper feeds from underside of roll.
  3. Click "Print Receipt" from recent orders history.
